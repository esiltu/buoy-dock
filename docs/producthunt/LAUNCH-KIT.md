## First comment

> Hi Product Hunt!
>
> I built Buoy because I wanted a dock that looks at home on macOS Tahoe but stays out of my way. Apple's Dock either sits on top of my windows or hides completely, and the nice alternatives all cost money.
>
> Buoy is a floating Liquid Glass dock:
>
> - It magnifies under your pointer and shows the app name above it
> - It slides away when a window reaches it, and comes back when you push the pointer to the bottom edge
> - It works in full-screen apps and stays hidden while you swipe between Spaces
> - Drag to reorder, drag up to remove, drop apps from Finder to add them
> - One click tucks Apple's Dock away, one click brings it back exactly as it was
>
> It's free and open source (MIT). No account, no tracking, no paid tier. Native Swift and SwiftUI, for Apple silicon and Intel on macOS 26 or later.
>
> One honest note: Buoy isn't notarized yet, so the first time you open it, click *Open Anyway* in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
>
> Try the dock in your browser: https://esiltu.github.io/buoy-dock/#demo
> Download and source: https://github.com/esiltu/buoy-dock
>
> I'd love your feedback: what's the one thing that would make you switch docks today? And if you like Buoy, a star on GitHub helps a lot.
